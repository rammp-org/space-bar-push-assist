4b. 4c. No friction path (a7 #2). Once a lean rests on the saddle, stick friction drags it along with the piston, and the cell loses the legs' y-stiffness x its own travel.
  a7's flat 2.5 mm saddle face could be held square by the contact (it needs ~116 N of normal force), so its legs worked fixed-guided: 110 N/mm, 13-33 N (reviewer a7). a8 crowns the face R1.2 across y: the contact is a line, the normal force acts through the crown's centre, and the saddle is free to turn. The legs then bend as free cantilevers loaded 7.85 mm above their tops: k = 2 / ((L^3/3 + h L^2 + h^2 L) / E I) = 11.2 N/mm.
  Shunt: 1.3 N at the 1.2 x FS travel of a dFS 0.10 cell, 3.4 N at the largest stop setting (0.30 mm). Rows, limit 10.
  Crown flattening: PLA yields at the crown under a heavy lean. After a 600 N lean the land is up to 0.92 mm wide (load 738 N, 10 mm of contact along the bore at 80 MPa), and a later lean holds up to N x 0.46 mm against the saddle's turn, which adds 0.0476 N per N.mm at the contact. At a 300 N lean (catch 184 N) the shunt is then 7.4 N at the largest setting (row, limit 10). While a 600 N lean is on one grip it can reach 19.6 N (capped by the guided value): the one case over the 10 N dead band (README).

== 5. Cell nuts: clamp, settling, creep (a4 #4, #5)
joint stiffness (stud 237, clamped PLA 58 kN/mm) 47 kN/mm: an M5 nut turned 60 deg (0.133 mm) past finger-tight would put 6.2 kN on a Ø12 boss (69 MPa): angle control is far too coarse for a 1 mm PLA boss.
  0.1 N.m (K 0.2) ->  100 N preload, boss  1.1 MPa
  0.2 N.m (K 0.2) ->  200 N preload, boss  2.2 MPa
  0.5 N.m (K 0.2) ->  500 N preload, boss  5.6 MPa
  1.0 N.m (K 0.2) -> 1000 N preload, boss 11.2 MPa
Spec: finger-tight with the bare 8 mm nut-driver bit held in the fingers (no handle, no drill): about 0.1-0.2 N.m, 100-200 N, 1.8-3.6 MPa on the boss. That holds the faces seated for push (push only adds to it) and for pull up to about the preload; past it a rear face can lift a few microns, which does not change the stud force the cell reads. A dab of hot glue over each nut stops it backing off. Settle overnight, re-snug, then set the stops.
creep term in the stop rows: 4.0 mm of PLA in the stop chain under clamp stress (two bosses + 1 mm under each) x 0.15 % over the unit's life = 0.0060 mm = 0.06 FS at dFS 0.10 (most of the creep at <= 4 MPa happens in the first day, before the stops are set).

== 6. Bench (cradle) vs fin
The jack is on the axis (3a). The block bears on the cradle's front wall round the same Ø16 counterbore as in the fin, so the push setting sees the same block. Pull: the cradle holds the block with two M4 lip screws at z -20 and the front wall at the block's foot instead of the fin's lips all round; contact compliance there lets the block turn by roughly 0.01 / 40 rad, which moves the pull contacts (z -6.5) by 0.0016 mm: the bench_support term (0.002 mm) in the stop rows.

== 10. Scale before stops (a5 #2): what an unset mV/V does, and the known-mass check window
  cell rated 1.0 mV/V with the firmware set to 1.0: its '120 %' is 120 % of true FS
  cell rated 1.5 mV/V with the firmware set to 1.0: its '120 %' is 80 % of true FS
  cell rated 2.0 mV/V with the firmware set to 1.0: its '120 %' is 60 % of true FS
  cell rated 3.0 mV/V with the firmware set to 1.0: its '120 %' is 40 % of true FS
  Known-mass check (cradle stood on its rear face, the mass hung from the jack screw): the flexure leaves carry kl / (kc + kl) of it beside the cell:
  20 kg: shunt 3.7 / 7.1 / 13.3 % at dFS 0.05 / 0.10 / 0.20, so a right scale reads 0.857-0.973 of the mass
  50 kg: shunt 1.5 / 3.0 / 5.8 % at dFS 0.05 / 0.10 / 0.20, so a right scale reads 0.932-0.995 of the mass
  PASS window 0.88-1.03 (a6 review #4: a6's 0.90 failed a right 20 kg cell at dFS 0.20); a 1.5x or 2x mV/V error reads 0.67 / 0.50 or 1.5 / 2.0: FAIL, and the stops are not set until it passes. A soft 20 kg cell (dFS over about 0.17) reads under 0.88 and fails falsely: calibrate the scale on the mass (safe). Calibrating on the mass in this setup reads grip force, so the stops land 1-15 % under 1.2 x FS of the cell itself: the safe side. a7: the cradle hangs from its eye on the jack axis and the mass hangs from the printed hanger on the jack head, so the cord, the jack and the mass are one vertical line (no books).
