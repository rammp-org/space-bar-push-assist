== G. Clamp creep vs stud stretch
  preload 100 N: stud stretch 0.42 um; clamped PLA compression 2.0 um (51 kN/mm). Creep of 0.15 % over 4 mm = 6 um > stretch -> preload goes to ~0 over life
  preload 200 N: stud stretch 0.84 um; clamped PLA compression 3.9 um (51 kN/mm). Creep of 0.15 % over 4 mm = 6 um > stretch -> preload goes to ~0 over life
  with no preload a few-um gap only lets the leaves (110 N/mm) take 110 x 0.006 = 0.7 N while it closes: negligible for the reading
